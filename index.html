About Me

I'm dual majoring in Computer Science (CS) and Digital Narrative and Interactive Design (DNID) and minoring in Information Science (IS) at the University of Pittsburgh. I'm in my Senior fall semester and will graduate in the spring.

Projects

Dahlia

I participated in the 2026 Games for Social Impact game jam and created Dahlia in a team of 3. It is a game that explores the idea of gender as a performance through controlling a wooden mannequin like a marionette in order to perform poses and score points. My team used the Godot game engine to create this videogame. I learned how to rapidly develop ideas with a team and work through crunch periods. I also learned more about GDscript, the native programming language for Godot.

Headless Parish

My DNID Capstone project adapts the Brazilian folklore myth of the "Headless Mule" into a first-person videogame. My team used Figma to brainstorm the narrative, Unity to create the game, and GitHub for a version control system. I learned how to brainstorm and iterate on videogame idea in a team, create intuitive systems for artists to use in the game engine, and communicate in a team effectively to accomplish our deliverables in a balanced and timely manner.
